Professional Referrals

KNP works with registered medical practitioners who wish to offer rTMS to their patients while retaining full clinical responsibility.

Work with KNP

KNP provides rTMS equipment, trained operators and session documentation. You can offer rTMS to your patients while keeping full charge of their care.

Continuity of Care

Diagnosis, suitability assessment and the decision to prescribe remain with you. You continue clinical management throughout the course of treatment. Your patient remains your patient.

A Coordinated Pathway

• Before the first session, a psychiatrist from New Town Specialists Medical Centre conducts an independent pre-treatment screening as an additional safeguard.

• Our operators, trained under Professor Kranz, deliver each session according to your approved protocol under clinical supervision on site.

• You receive session summaries at agreed intervals. 

Technical Depth

Professor Georg Kranz, founder of KNP, has worked with TMS across research, laboratory and professional-education settings for more than 20 years. He and his team would be pleased to discuss equipment, protocol implementation and referral arrangements in as much depth as you like.
